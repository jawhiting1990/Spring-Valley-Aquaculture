About Us

Spring Valley Aquaculture LLC is an indoor fish, chicken, and rabbit farm located inside the converted former Meeme Elementary Charter School in Newton, Wisconsin. Operated by Russ Albert II and family, the farm raises tilapia and other livestock in controlled indoor environments without antibiotics, hormones, or added chemicals. By feeding their tilapia a specialized algae-based diet, they produce a remarkably clean, mild-tasting fish sold fresh and live right from the farm. Originally, the company did shrimp farming, but their focus shifted to fish and other livestock. Open year-round for local pickup and in-person sales. Because harvest schedules vary for indoor tanks, call ahead to check current availability. Reach Russ Albert at (920) 980-3767, by email, or check harvest updates on the Spring Valley Aquaculture Facebook page and its Eat Midwest Fish directory listing.


Albert's Hydrovac

Stephanie and Russ also operate Albert's Hydrovac, LLC, and Cascade Septic, LLC. Albert's Hydrovac LLC provides non-destructive hydro-excavation, construction site hauling, and septic pumping across Manitowoc, Sheboygan, and neighboring Wisconsin counties. Russ' Cascade Septic, LLC is a residential and commercial septic and holding tank pumping company serving Manitowoc, Sheboygan, and neighboring eastern Wisconsin counties. You can check availability or schedule a truck directly with Russ Albert at (920) 980-3767 or through the Albert's Hydrovac LLC Facebook page.
